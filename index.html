Input format and limitations
{"schema_version":1,"records":[{"id":"DEMO-12","ru":"От 120 000 USD, 42 м²","en":"From USD 120,000, 42 sqm"}]}

Number matching ignores order and repeated occurrences. It cannot detect every incorrect assignment of a number to a fact, interpret contracts, compare arbitrary dates or understand every qualifier. Status vocabulary is deliberately small. Review the full text even with no detected differences. Exported review records identify the exact text pair, not a cryptographically authenticated signer.
